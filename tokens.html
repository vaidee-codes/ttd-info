Before leaving for a counter

Bring the essentials. Verify the details.

  1. Check the current arrangement

    Ask which counter is issuing your token type and whether each traveller must be present. Do not assume a fixed daily start time.

  2. Bring original identification

    The community reference lists original Aadhaar and in-person attendance. Confirm accepted ID and eligibility with TTD, particularly for children or visitors without Aadhaar.

  3. Read the token before travelling uphill

    Follow the reporting date, time and location printed on your own token. Keep the token and ID accessible.

  4. Confirm DD walking and scanning instructions

    The reference describes Srivari Mettu and a scan at the 1,200th step. It also names ATGH Circle for reporting. These operational details are not independently confirmed as current; ask TTD staff before relying on them.

Know where each status comes from

The SSD card reads the slot, date and balance published on TTD’s official homepage. DD has no official public balance; community pages provide independent observations. Check the timestamp, then verify with staff before travelling.
