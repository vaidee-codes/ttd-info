Extension support · v6.8.0

Let’s get your autofill working.

Try the quick checks below. If the TTD page has changed or a supported field still fails, send a useful, privacy-safe report.

Start here

Five checks solve most issues

1. Update the extension

Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode and choose Update. The current published version is 6.8.0.

2. Use the matching tab

Choose Pilgrims, individual Seva, Group Seva or Srivani to match the TTD page you opened.

3. Wait for the page

Let the TTD form and its dropdowns finish loading, then open the extension and click the relevant fill action.

4. Refresh after an update

Reload the TTD tab after Chrome updates or after installing a new extension version.

5. Verify every field

Autofill is an entry aid. Review names, IDs, dates, dropdowns and files before you submit or pay.

TTD page unavailable?

Availability, login, CAPTCHA and payment problems are controlled by TTD or its providers, not by the extension.

Common questions

Workflow-specific fixes

Some pilgrim slots are not filled+

The extension intentionally detects available slots and fills only the number present. Confirm that the saved list is in your preferred order and that the TTD page has rendered all slots before trying again.

Passport popup does not complete+

Check that country, passport number, Visa/OCI number, visa type and validity date are complete and valid. Close any half-open popup, refresh the booking page and retry after it fully loads.

Photo or document upload fails+

For supported Srivari Seva uploads, use JPEG or PNG within the TTD form's current size limit (the published workflow currently specifies up to 1 MB). Re-select the file in the extension if it was moved or removed from your device.

A Group Seva member fills the wrong section+

Confirm the member number in the extension matches the accordion on the TTD page. Member 1 is the team leader. Fill one member at a time and verify before allowing auto-advance.

Srivani fills fewer than expected+

Srivani supports up to 4 saved people and fills the rows available on the current form. It cannot create additional ticket inventory or bypass the booking limit.

Saved details disappeared+

Data is stored in Chrome's local extension storage for that browser profile. Removing the extension or clearing its storage deletes it. It is not synced to a developer account or recoverable from our servers.

Passes & payments

How the Professional Pass works

What the pass covers

The pass unlocks one-click autofilling of the TTD pilgrim-booking form. Srivani and other saved details remain free. Choose a 7-day (₹99), 30-day (₹299) or 90-day (₹699) pass at ttd-info.vercel.app/pass.

One browser at a time

Each pass key activates on a single browser. A key that is already active on another browser can't be activated again — use it where it was first activated, or remove the pass there first.

Renewals stack fairly

Buying another pass while one is active adds its remaining days to your current expiry instead of replacing it. The timer in the extension always shows the effective (stacked) expiry.

Automatic activation

Open the pass page from the extension and, after a successful payment, the pass activates in that browser automatically. You can also copy and paste the pass key into the extension at any time.

Refunds & returns

Digital pass keys are non-returnable once used. If you haven't used a pass, request a refund within 24 hours of purchase and it will be returned to your original payment method. See Refund Policy.

Payment trouble

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments. For a failed payment, a missing key, or a receipt, email ttdautofill@gmail.com with the order reference and we'll sort it out.

What to include

  • Extension version and Chrome version
  • Which extension tab you used
  • The TTD page/service name
